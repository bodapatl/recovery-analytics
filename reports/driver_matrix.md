## Dimension notes

>[!Note] Language effects: the contract has **no language column**; borrower state is used as the nearest market/segment proxy and language itself is reported **N/A**. Client effects: there is **no client column** either; `loan_type` (with state) is the client-mix proxy.
**DPD band** — recovery quality is mostly a function of how delinquent the account is
**Loan type (client proxy)** — no client column exists in the contract; loan_type+state are the closest usable proxies
**Risk segment** — assigned risk bucket as sold by the risk team
**Geography (borrower state)** — no language column exists in the data — state is the closest geography/language-market proxy (marked N/A for language effects)
**Agent tenure band** — tenure_months_at_start (join date → Jan-2026) spans 19–24 months across all 1,000 agents: **almost no variance**, so tenure effects are *unidentifiable from this data*, not merely flat. (Agent-team/diversity fixes from master identity resolution remain valid.) Needed: a workforce with real tenure dispersion before this driver is testable.
**Campaign strategy** — the honest causal test is the exact-matched DiD (see memo §4): +₹895/acct/month, 95% CI [−₹1,224, +₹3,013], p=0.40 **— no detectable lift**. Version-level raw shares are Correlation at best.
**Channel economy** — raw WhatsApp event counts (PAYMENT_CLICK ≈ SENT, lifecycle ~1:1) cannot yield a meaningful per-message conversion rate, so none is claimed; usable economics are cash-per-sent-message (~₹3,943) and unit cost → the voice attempt unit (~₹73) is ~365× the digital message unit (₹0.20); voice consumes 100% of modeled spend for a flat trend. Field visits have no cost source (assumption knob absent) — N/A.
**Telephony vendor** — vendor codes normalized in 02_clean; connection-rate spread 2.3 pp. But recovery-vs-connect-rate elasticity is not identified in this data (no dialer experiment) — routing spend on vendor choice is a Hypothesis, not an evidence-backed lever.
**Calling hour** — after tz normalization the hour bands differ by 0.8 pp RPC. Selection bias risk: best borrowers may be callable earlier — treat as directional, verify with an A/B on dialer time windows before re-sequencing the day.
**Attempt frequency** — pay-rate climbs with attempts, but this is largely selection (dialers pile attempts on promising accounts). The DiD's null strategy effect suggests frequency alone is not the lever; test with a capped-attempt experiment before buying more dialer capacity.
